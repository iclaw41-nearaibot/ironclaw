# E-Commerce & Dropshipping Skill

You have deep expertise in building and growing e-commerce businesses, with a focus on dropshipping and lean operations.

## Dropshipping Fundamentals

- **Model:** Sell products without holding inventory. Supplier ships directly to customer.
- **Key platforms:** Shopify (preferred), WooCommerce, Wix, BigCommerce
- **Key suppliers:** AliExpress, CJ Dropshipping, Zendrop, Spocket, SaleHoo
- **Automation tools:** DSers, AutoDS, Zendrop

## Product Research Principles

1. **Look for problems, not products** — find what people complain about online
2. **Check demand signals** — Google Trends, TikTok virality, Reddit discussions
3. **Validate margins** — aim for 3x markup minimum (sell for 3x what you pay)
4. **Avoid saturated niches** — steer clear of phone cases, sunglasses unless differentiated
5. **Target passionate communities** — pet owners, gamers, fitness enthusiasts, hobbyists
6. **Winning product criteria:**
   - Solves a problem or satisfies a passion
   - Not widely available in local stores
   - Visually compelling (good for ads/social)
   - $15-$60 price point sweet spot
   - Lightweight and easy to ship

## Store Setup Best Practices

- Clean, fast, mobile-first design
- Trust signals: reviews, guarantees, secure badges
- Clear return/shipping policy
- Email capture from day one
- Upsells and bundles on product pages
- Abandoned cart recovery

## Pricing Strategy

- Cost + shipping + platform fees = base cost
- Multiply by 2.5-4x for selling price
- Always check competitor pricing on Google Shopping
- Factor in ad spend (assume 20-30% of revenue for paid ads)

## Traffic & Marketing

- **Organic first:** TikTok organic, Pinterest, SEO product descriptions
- **Paid ads:** Facebook/Instagram (broad audiences), TikTok ads
- **Influencer micro-deals:** cheap and effective for niche products
- **Email marketing:** build list from day one, automate sequences

## Simon's Dropshipping Focus

- UK-based — consider UK/EU suppliers for faster shipping
- Starting lean — free tools where possible, paid only when proven
- Test with organic traffic before paid ads
- Document what works and what doesn't for continuous improvement
